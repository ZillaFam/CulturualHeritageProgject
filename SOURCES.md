## Which facts came from where (for your chart notes)

- **Britannica — Chinese New Year / Lunar New Year:** 15 days ending at the
  Lantern Festival; lucky foods and their meanings (fish = surplus, dumplings =
  wealth); red envelopes from elders; lion & dragon dances.
- **Britannica — Dragon Boat Festival:** Qu Yuan, poet-minister of Chu in the
  Warring States period; drowned in the Miluo River; boats, drums and rice to
  protect him; zongzi; 5th day of 5th lunar month; UNESCO Intangible Cultural
  Heritage list (2009, first Chinese festival).
- **History.com:** New Year traditions — cleaning before (not on!) New Year's
  Day, red decorations, couplets, the upside-down fú wordplay, hóngbāo /
  yāsuìqián lucky money.
- **USC Digital Folklore Archives:** the Nian monster legend — fears red, light,
  and noise; "Guo Nian" = passing over the year/beast.
- **Smithsonian Magazine:** mooncake history — Tang dynasty court gifts, Song
  dynasty records, round shape = reunion (tuányuán), the Ming "secret messages
  in mooncakes" rebellion legend; Chang'e and Hou Yi.
